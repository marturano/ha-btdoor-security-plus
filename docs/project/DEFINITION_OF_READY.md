# Definition of Ready

Work may enter implementation only when:

- the roadmap item or approved addition is identified;
- scope, acceptance criteria and non-goals are explicit;
- affected architecture, API, entities, translations, security, compatibility and documentation are identified;
- static-analysis, unit, integration, regression and benchmark needs are defined;
- automation/blueprint/dashboard impacts are classified;
- migration and release-documentation impacts are known;
- an ADR is created or explicitly marked not required;
- UX, accessibility, compatibility and publication impacts are classified;
- affected workflow gates and required dashboard domains are identified;
- risks and dependencies are recorded in the Engineering Notebook.
