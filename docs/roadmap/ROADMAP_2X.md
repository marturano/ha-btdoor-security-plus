# Roadmap 2.x — BTicino / Netatmo Ecosystem Platform

## 2.0 — Ecosystem Foundation
Status: Foundation documented in RC.15 Phase 3; runtime adapters remain incremental.

Unified account, home, room, module and capability model; Home + Control, Home + Security, Netatmo and MyHOME bridges; multi-home and multi-account; shared diagnostics and migration strategy.

## 2.1 — Connected Electrical System
Living Now with Netatmo, Livinglight with Netatmo, MatixGO with Netatmo, gateways, lights, sockets, dimmers, relays, shutters, wireless controls and scenes.

## 2.2 — BTDoor Energy+
BTDIN devices, metering, load shedding, large-load control, tariff profiles, Energy Dashboard, photovoltaic/storage coordination and overload alerts.

## 2.3 — BTDoor Climate+
Smarther, Smarther2, Netatmo thermostats and valves, HVAC modes, zones, schedules, away/antifreeze/boost, open-window logic and energy-aware control.

## 2.4 — Weather & Environment
Weather stations, indoor/outdoor modules, rain, wind, temperature, humidity, CO2, pressure, noise, air quality and comfort intelligence.

## 2.5 — Unified Security
Cameras, sirens, door/window sensors, smoke/CO detectors, alarm panels, BTicino/MyHOME security, incident timeline and privacy-aware event handling.

## 2.6 — Connected Access & Video Door Entry
Generalized support for Classe 300EOS, Classe 100X and compatible systems, calls, media, intercom, relays, users, permissions and temporary access.

## 2.7 — MyHOME Platform
Gateway matrix, lighting, shutters, climate, energy, scenes, security, video door entry, bus diagnostics and non-destructive migration to the unified model.

## 2.8 — Unified Home Platform
Cross-domain scenes and automations, whole-home status, unified dashboard/timeline/notifications, profiles and coherent exposure to Assist, Alexa/Alexa+ and Google Assistant.

## 2.9 — Local-First & Resilience
Local discovery/control where possible, cache, offline degradation, deferred synchronization, cloud/local fallback, backup/restore, certificates and health monitoring.
